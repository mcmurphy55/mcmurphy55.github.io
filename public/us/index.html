Us is an iPhone app for sitting with someone you love, asking one question, and keeping the story. Add your family, roll the dice for a question, record, and each story goes into that person’s album.

Questions

Which iPhones does Us work on?
iPhones with iOS 26 or later. Summaries and the list of people and places mentioned while recording need Apple Intelligence. Transcripts need Apple’s speech model, which iOS downloads when it is first needed.

What is free, and what does Unlock Us add?
Stories with yourself and with the first family member you record with are free, with their transcripts, summaries and About pages. So are the family tree, listening, exporting, iCloud, and albums shared with you. Unlock Us, one purchase and never a subscription, adds stories with everyone else in your family and sharing albums. Through Family Sharing, everyone in your family group gets it too.

I bought Unlock Us. How do I get it on another iPhone?
In Us, open Settings → Unlock Us and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Where are my stories kept?
In Us on your iPhone and, while Settings → iCloud → Keep in iCloud is on, in your private iCloud. Sign in to a new iPhone with the same Apple Account and your library comes with it. See the privacy policy.

How do I share an album with my family?
At the foot of someone’s album, tap Share <name>’s album and send the invitation. The people you invite open it in Us on their own iPhones, signed in to iCloud. They can listen and read, but cannot change it, and your notes are never shared.

Can I keep a copy outside Us?
Yes. At the foot of someone’s album, Export <name>’s stories makes one ZIP of their stories, with the original recordings, transcripts and, if you choose, photos. It opens on any computer, without Us.

Recording stopped. What happened?
A phone call, Siri, another app’s sound, or unplugging the microphone stops it. Everything recorded until then is kept. Us never starts again by itself: tap Resume recording when everyone is ready.
