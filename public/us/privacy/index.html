Privacy Policy

Last updated November 3, 2026

Us has no account, no server of its own, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer never receives your recordings, photos, stories or the people in your family tree.

What Us keeps, and where

Us keeps what you make in it: the people you add (their names, the pictures you choose, how they are related, and years of birth and death), the stories you record (recordings, notes, photos, the questions asked, and the moments you mark), their transcripts and summaries, About pages, the questions you save, and your settings.

  • On your iPhone. Everything is kept in Us on your iPhone.
  • In your private iCloud. While Keep in iCloud is on (it is on unless you turn it off), the same library is kept in the private iCloud of the Apple Account signed in on your iPhone, so it appears on your other iPhones. It is stored by Apple in your account; the developer cannot read it. You can turn it off in Us under Settings → iCloud.

Microphone, camera and photos

Us uses the microphone only to record, after you tap Start recording. Recording goes on if you lock the screen or leave Us, until you stop it; a phone call pauses it. The camera is used only when you take a photo for a story, and only the photos you choose from your library are added.

Transcripts, summaries and Apple Intelligence

  • Transcripts are made on your iPhone with Apple’s speech recognition.
  • While recording, Us may list the people and places mentioned so far. Apple Intelligence writes the list on your iPhone; the words it heard are forgotten when you stop, and nothing of it is saved.
  • Summaries are written on your iPhone by Apple Intelligence.
  • About pages, the check for misheard words, and summaries of stories too long for your iPhone use Apple’s Private Cloud Compute, only once you write an About page or turn Use Private Cloud Compute on. The text of the transcripts concerned, with the names in them, is then sent to Apple’s Private Cloud Compute. Apple says data sent there is used only to fulfill the request and is neither stored nor made accessible to Apple. The developer never receives it. You can turn it off in Us under Settings → Apple Intelligence.
  • Translations are made on your iPhone with Apple’s Translation.

Sharing an album

Nothing is shared unless you share an album. When you do, a copy of that person’s album is put in your iCloud and shared, through Apple’s iCloud sharing, with the people you invite: their saved stories with recordings and photos, transcripts, summaries, About page, the names and pictures of who told them, and covers. Your notes are never included. The people you invite can listen and read in Us on their own iPhones, but cannot change anything. You can stop sharing at any time; the copy is then removed from iCloud, and from their iPhones the next time they open it.

Exports

When you export an album, Us makes a ZIP file on your iPhone and you choose where it goes. What happens to it then depends on where you send it.

Purchases

Unlock Us is sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; Us learns only whether the purchase is active on your Apple Account.

Recording other people

Everyone you record should know and agree. In some places the law requires the consent of everyone in a conversation before it is recorded. Us reminds you before you record, but getting that agreement is up to you.

Children

Us is meant to be used by adults. Families may record stories with their children; those stories stay in the family’s own library as described above, and the developer never receives them.

Deleting your data

  • Delete a story or a person in Us, and it is removed from your iPhone and from your iCloud.
  • Settings → Reset Us erases the library on your iPhone and, if you choose Erase here and in iCloud, in iCloud too.
  • Deleting the app removes what is on your iPhone. What is in iCloud can also be deleted in the Settings app, under your Apple Account’s iCloud storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
