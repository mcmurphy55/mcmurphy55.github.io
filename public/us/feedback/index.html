The 10-minute test, if you haven’t tried it yet
  1. Choose a picture and tap This is me.
  2. On Home, tap + and add one family member.
  3. Tap their picture, or yours, then Roll the dice and Ask this question.
  4. Record a story, with them or by yourself. Tap Stop & review, then Save story.
  5. Open the album and read the transcript.
